Fevro Browser Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Fevro Browser (“the app”) is a web browser for Android, published by Ash (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Installations to understand how the app is used and to keep it working. Through these services, the app collects:

InformationWhy
App instance ID and Firebase installation ID (random identifiers generated for each install)Count unique installs and users, and measure retention
Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app versionFix compatibility problems and improve the app
Usage events: app opens, screen views, and similar interactions with the appLearn which features are used
Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID or Amazon advertising ID, where available)Collected by Firebase Analytics for attribution and measurement
IP address, used by Google to infer approximate location such as country or cityUnderstand where the app is used. Google does not store the full IP address in Analytics reports.

The app does not request access to your device’s GPS location, contacts, camera roll, microphone, or accounts. It does not require you to create an account.

What we do not collect

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads in the app. We do not use the information above to identify you by name.

Your browsing data

Browsing history, cookies, bookmarks, and saved settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them on our servers. Websites you visit have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Third-party services

Firebase is provided by Google. Google processes the information listed above on our behalf and under its own terms. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

How long we keep it

Firebase Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device’s settings.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all further collection.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this address and update the effective date above.
