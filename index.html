1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fevro Browser ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We designed the App to keep your browsing data on your device. We do not run our own servers for your browsing data, and we do not sell your personal information.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Browsing history: page titles, addresses (URLs) and visit times.
  • Bookmarks and Speed Dial: titles, URLs and folders you create.
  • Downloads: file names, source URLs, file types, progress and the location of files you download.
  • Settings and preferences: theme, search engine choice (including custom search engines you add), HTTPS-Only Mode, clear-on-exit options and similar settings.
  • SwiftShield content-blocking data: your enabled filter lists, custom blocking rules and the list of sites you have allowed.
  • Cookies, cache and site data stored by the built-in web engine (WebView) while you browse.

You can delete history, downloads, cookies and cache at any time from the App's settings, or by clearing the App's storage in Android settings. Pages opened in an Incognito tab are not saved to your history.

3. Information collected automatically (analytics)

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve it. Firebase may collect:

  • Basic app usage events, such as app opens, daily activity, screens viewed and download actions.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate region.
  • An app instance identifier generated by Firebase and, where available on your device, your Android Advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, which Google uses to determine approximate location and does not store as part of event data.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy information. We use it only for analytics and app improvement. The App does not display third-party advertising.

You can limit this collection by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), or by contacting us to request deletion (see Section 12).

4. Information sent to third parties when you use the App

As a web browser, the App connects to the internet on your behalf. This necessarily shares some information with the services you choose to use:

  • Websites you visit receive your IP address, device and browser details and any information you submit, just as in any browser. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Search engines. When you search, your query is sent to the search engine you select (for example Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave Search, Kagi, or a custom engine you add).
  • Search suggestions. If search suggestions are enabled, what you type in the address bar may be sent to the suggestion service of your selected engine (for example Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Brave) and to Wikipedia to provide suggestions. You can turn suggestions off in settings.
  • Site icons. The App may request website icons (favicons) from Google's favicon service, which receives the domain of the site.
  • Start page news. The start page may load headlines from public news feeds (for example BBC News and Google News).
  • Content-blocking lists. SwiftShield downloads public blocklists from third-party hosts (for example StevenBlack hosts, Peter Lowe's ad server list and the Firebog AdGuard list). These requests do not include your browsing history.
  • Translation. If you use page translation, the page address is sent to Google Translate.

We do not control these third parties and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the following Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load web pages and check connectivity.
  • Wake lock: to keep downloads running while your screen is off.
  • Advertising ID and ad services access: used by Firebase Analytics as described in Section 3.

The App does not request access to your contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, camera, precise location or device storage permissions.

6. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the App, fix bugs, understand which features are used, and keep the App secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your browsing history for advertising or profiling.

7. Data retention and security

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. Analytics data held by Google Firebase is retained according to the retention settings configured for the App and Google's policies. We use industry-standard protections and offer an HTTPS-Only Mode that upgrades insecure connections where possible. Some websites may still use unencrypted connections, and no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because browsing data is stored only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For analytics data, contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

Google may process analytics data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
